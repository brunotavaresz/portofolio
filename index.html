About Me


I hold a Bachelor's in Computer Science Engineering from the University of Aveiro and I'm now in the second year of the Master's in Informatics Engineering.

As a student researcher at IEETA, I built a webcam-based eye-tracking platform to study where people look on screens, physical objects and around a room. My master's thesis, Reading the Room, explores how cameras installed in a space can give interactive systems context about the people and objects around them.

I'm also a certified trainer (CCP, IEFP), and I'm looking for opportunities to keep growing in research and software engineering.
